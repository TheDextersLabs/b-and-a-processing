What We Take Care Of

We handle your harvest cleanly, professionally, and exactly to your custom cut specifications. While we primarily serve the surrounding counties right around Aragon (including Polk, Bartow, Floyd, Paulding, and Haralson), everyone is welcome to bring their deer in.

  • Venison Steaks & Backstraps: Premium cuts prepared right for the grill or skillet.
  • Sausage & Specialty Blends: Savory breakfast sausage, jalapeño blends, and summer sausage.
  • Flanks, Roasts & Cuts: Carefully trimmed roasts, tenderloins, and shank cuts.
  • Ground Venison & Burger Trim: Clean, high-yield burger grinds and custom trim packaging.
  • Vacuum Sealing: Heavy-duty freezer-ready packaging to keep your meat fresh all year.
