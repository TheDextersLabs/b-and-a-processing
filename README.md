## About B & A Processing
**B & A Processing** is a premier custom meat and wild game processing facility dedicated to helping hunters and local producers keep their families fed while supporting ethical wildlife population management. We stand behind clean, healthy, self-sustaining living that honors the natural circle of life—because there is nothing quite like the peace of mind that comes from knowing you had a direct hand in the meat sitting on your family's dinner table.

### Species We Process
While **white-tailed deer (venison)** is our primary focus, our facility is fully equipped to process a wide range of game and livestock, including:
* **Deer & Wild Game**
* **Feral Hog & Domestic Pork (Pig)**
* **Beef (Cattle)**
* **Poultry (Chickens, Turkeys, and more)**

---

## Our Standards & Specialty Products
Sanitation and **fast turnarounds** are the backbone of our reputation. Every harvest is handled in a meticulously sanitized work area, aged and stored in clean, well-maintained walk-in freezers, and sealed using commercial-grade packaging practices so your cuts stay fresh all year.

Beyond standard prime cuts, steaks, and flanks, we operate top-tier commercial sausage-making and smoking equipment using locally sourced spices distributed right here in our community. Our signature offerings include:
* **Bryce’s Famous Jalapeño Breakfast Sausage** (crafted with Bryce's proprietary blend of flavors and spices)
* **Smacking Summer Sausage & Custom Smoked Meats**
* **Hamburger-Style Ground Meats & Pure Ground Venison**
* **Custom Steaks, Roasts, and Flanks**

### How Pricing & Cut Selection Work
Because every harvest and custom order is unique, **final pricing is discussed in person after weighing your animal and reviewing your processing options**. This hands-on consultation lets you decide exactly how your harvest is broken down—choosing what stays as prime steaks and roasts versus what gets ground into burger or crafted into summer and breakfast sausage. 

*Note: Freezer and processing spaces fill up fast during peak season, and our team works tirelessly to deliver the highest-quality cuts possible for your game.*

## Information Security & Zero-Tracking Privacy Policy
> *"We're here for the meats—not the information off your phone."*

This web portal is engineered with a strict **Privacy-by-Design** architecture:
1. **Zero Digital Data Collection:** Outside of the basic intake details physically required at the shop to tag and process your animal, this website collects **zero personal data**.
2. **No Financial Storage:** We do not process, capture, or hold credit card numbers or banking credentials on this server. Clicking a payment button hands you off directly to the encrypted environment of your chosen payment app (Zelle, Cash App, or Venmo).
3. **No Tracking or Fingerprinting:** This static site does not log IP addresses, fingerprint mobile devices, or deploy tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party data harvesters.
